Privacy Policy for Mushaf Classic

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Mushaf Classic ("the app") is a Quran reading app. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information We Collect

The app does not require account creation, login, or any personal information to use.

The app stores the following data locally on your device only (never transmitted to us or any third party):

  • Your last-read page, so you can resume where you left off
  • Bookmarked pages
  • Your preferred translation, tafsir source, app language, and theme (day/night) settings
  • Whether you've completed the app's onboarding tour

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Third-Party Content Services

To display Quran text, translations, tafsir, and audio recitation, the app fetches content from these public services over the internet:

  • quran.com — Quran text, translations, tafsir, search
  • everyayah.com — recitation audio
  • quranwbw.com — word-by-word audio

These requests fetch public religious content and do not include any personal information about you.

Permissions

The app may request audio-related permissions solely to enable playback of Quran recitation audio through your device's speaker/media system. The app does not record audio, access your microphone for any feature, or capture any audio input from you.

Advertising & Analytics

The app does not display ads, and does not use any analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting service. We do not collect, sell, or share any user data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since no personal information is collected at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
